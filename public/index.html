Your node↔ Signed HTTPS ↔Trusted peer

Connect your deployments

  1. Give each deployment a unique NODE_ID and its own ADMIN_TOKEN.
  2. Set the same long NETWORK_SECRET on trusted nodes.
  3. Set PEER_URLS to one or more other nodes’ HTTPS origins (Render, Railway, Vercel, Netlify, Fly or a custom domain). Further nodes are discovered automatically by gossip, hello announcements and the shared Supabase registry.
  4. Attach persistent storage and restart. Sync runs while the service is awake.

Free Render services can sleep and lose local data on restarts or redeploys. Use the included persistent-disk blueprint for durable learning. Multiple accounts do not pool compute or train one distributed language model.
